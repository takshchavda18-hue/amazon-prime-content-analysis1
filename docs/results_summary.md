# Results summary

Dataset: amazon_prime_titles.csv (Kaggle: shivamb / amazon-prime-movies-and-tv-shows)

- Rows x columns (raw): 9,668 x 12

## Key insights

- The catalogue has 9,668 titles: 80.8% Movies and 19.2% TV Shows.
- 56.0% of titles were released in 2015 or later.
- Most titles were added in 2021 (among titles that have a date_added).
- Top countries: United States, India, United Kingdom.
- Top genres: Drama, Comedy, Action.
- The two most common ratings are 13+, 16+ (39.3% of rated titles).
- Median movie length is 91 minutes.
- 72.4% of TV shows have only 1 season.
- Data quality: date_added is known for only 1.6% of titles and country for 7.0%, so date and country charts describe a subset of the catalogue.
